Vireo

I'm building a sustainability scanner app called Vireo. It helps people make more informed shopping decisions by letting them scan or search everyday products to see how sustainable they really are. Many brands use vague "green" language, but consumers don't have an easy way to verify what's actually eco-friendly - this is where I hope to step in.

My vision is to pull from open databases and product sources to score items based on packaging, ingredients, certifications, and evnironmental impact. Over time, I hope to incorporate community feedback and alternatives, making it easier to choose products that align with your own values - whether that be products that are more eco-friendly, cruelty-free, or fair trade, without needing to become an expert.
