CAKE: Context-Aware KV Evictions

This was my final project for CS525 Advanced Distributed Systems at University of Illinois - Urbana Champaign, and my research partner and I are working on getting this paper published at a top conference or journal.

As Large Language Models (LLMs) take on increasingly complex tasks, their input is supplemented with more extended contexts that incorporate domain knowledge. Using such long contexts often poses severe memory bottlenecks. The context processing delay can be alleviated by using the KV cache, but fetching the KV cache, which contains large tensors and often exceeds the model's size, can cause high latency and network delays and completely exhaust GPU RAM.

CAKE (Context-Aware KV Enhancement) is a Reinforcement Learning (RL) based framework to optimize KV Caching policies. The CAKE agent selects from various actions at certain steps in generation - including quantizing a cache layer to lower precision, or one of several eviction actions. We train and evaluate our model using Llama-3.2-3B-Instruct using conversational generation with realistic prompts, and the agent learns policy to minimize memory while maintaining generation quality.
